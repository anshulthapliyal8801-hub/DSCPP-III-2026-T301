Welcome to MUNSphere

This is a simple Model United Nations (MUN) Conference Management System.

Use the sidebar to navigate through different modules:

  • Delegates - View and manage conference delegates
  • Committees - Manage committees and their capacity
  • Sessions - Create and manage conference sessions
  • Points - Raise and rule on Points of Information (POI) and Points of Order (POO)
  • Reports - View conference statistics and reports
